A · Everything eases in

The classic startup look: each heading and card fades up, staggered. Familiar and easy, but it's motion on every scroll, which is what motion-sensitive people find tiring. It's also the most "template-y".

B · One "show-off" moment

One section pins in place and plays a short story tied to the scrollbar: raise, point, pinch, click. Scroll back up and it rewinds. The rest of the page stays calm. This is what the taste skill recommends and what most award sites actually do.
